Interactive demo to browse through data, generations and evaluations from the paper Clarify, Abstain or Answer? Strategising in Conversation with Belief-Augmented Generation.

Each record shows one question and its references from AmbigQA, the assistant LLM's belief state (K sampled candidate answers), the output of belief-augmented generation (a strategy, reasoning trace, and response), the resulting potentially multi-turn conversation with a simulated user, reference-based LLM-judge verdicts, and baselines.

Pick an assistant model, augmentation method (SAG is a prompt-only method, BAG1-3 exploit the belief state), and whether to apply them again after clarification in turn 4. You can also pick a brevity-inducing prompt to shorten generations (and thus also the belief state). · Code · Data
